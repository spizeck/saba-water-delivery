# Production Handover

[Home](Home.md) · [Government Production Readiness milestone](https://github.com/spizeck/saba-water-delivery/milestone/1)

The milestone moves the service from a developer-owned technical pilot to operation that Public Entity Saba can administer and recover independently. A working application is one part of that outcome. Account control, credentials, recovery, monitoring, and demonstrated staff capability must also be established.

**Reviewed 12 September 2026:** all eight issues below were open. Their descriptions document the pilot ownership and required transition; they are not completion evidence. This review did not inspect private cloud configuration or verify backup activation. No migration or government acceptance is claimed here.

The final drill is the intended acceptance gate, after the core ownership work is substantially complete. It must use safe recovery targets or simulations, preserve live service, and expose gaps as tracked work. No deadline or acceptance date is set by this draft.
