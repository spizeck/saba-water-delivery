# Driver Guide

[Home](Home.md) · Screen instructions: [canonical Driver Guide](https://github.com/spizeck/saba-water-delivery/blob/main/docs/DRIVER_GUIDE.md)

## Become available

Sign in and open the Driver portal. Your account must be linked to the government Driver Registry and marked eligible. Set yourself online when ready to work and offline when finished. Availability is your working preference; eligibility is a separate government decision. Going offline does not finish a delivery already assigned to you.

If access is restricted or your account is not linked, contact the Water Delivery Office. Installing the web app does not create a driver account or grant eligibility. The app needs connectivity for offers and updates.

## Respond to offers

Normal dispatch shows at most one offer at a time, not a list to choose from. Review the village, quantity, directions, and any notes. **Accept Delivery** claims the request if it is still available. An offer itself does not reserve it, so another assignment may make an old offer unavailable.

**Decline** passes on the request. Reaching the administrator-set daily decline limit pauses new offers and forces you offline for the displayed cooldown. This does not remove government eligibility. Follow the app's Saba-local availability message before returning online.

## Collect and deliver all loads

Record **Water collected** for each physical load, choosing the actual fill station and checking the displayed assigned meter. Contact the office if the meter assignment is missing. One load is 1,000 gallons; a two-load request needs two collection records and 2,000 gallons delivered.

Use **Mark Delivered** only after the full requested quantity physically reaches the customer. Collection is not delivery, and there is no half-delivered request status. If you cannot finish, contact dispatch to coordinate the remaining work; do not mark the whole request delivered merely to clear your screen.

Delivered means your physical work on that request is complete. The resident's later confirmation or dispute is separate. You can receive another normal offer when no claimed work remains and you meet the normal availability conditions; waiting for resident confirmation or an email does not hold you up.

## Delivery Runs

A dispatcher may deliberately assign several requests together as a Delivery Run. They appear as individual deliveries, possibly supported by a printed run sheet. Record each load's collection and each request's full delivery separately. There is no whole-run delivery button.

Normal offers pause while claimed work remains in the run. Once physical work is finished, a run can still show **Awaiting Confirmation** without keeping you busy. If connectivity prevents app updates, coordinate with dispatch so staff can verify and record the work you actually completed. See [Dispatcher Guide](Dispatcher-Guide.md).
