# Driver Guide

[Home](Home.md) · Screen instructions: [canonical Driver Guide](https://github.com/spizeck/saba-water-delivery/blob/main/docs/DRIVER_GUIDE.md)

## Become available

Sign in and open the Driver portal. Your account must be linked to the government Driver Registry and marked eligible. Set yourself online when ready to work and offline when finished. Availability is your working preference; eligibility is a separate government decision. Going offline returns a pending assigned delivery to dispatch (counts toward your release limit) — committed work with recorded water collection blocks going offline until it is completed or resolved by the water office.

If access is restricted or your account is not linked, contact the Water Delivery Office. Installing the web app does not create a driver account or grant eligibility. The app needs connectivity for offers and updates.

## Your assigned delivery

Normal dispatch assigns you one delivery at a time, not a list to choose from. The delivery shown to you is **already assigned to you** — there is no Accept step, and closing the app does not release it. Review the village, quantity, directions, and any notes, then collect and deliver.

When the driver workflow changes in an important way, the portal shows a one-time notice explaining the change. Press **Got it** to confirm you have read it — this is recorded on your driver record once, across all your devices, and never affects whether deliveries are assigned to you.

If you cannot or will not make the delivery, use **Decline / Release Delivery** to return it to dispatch for another driver. Reaching the administrator-set daily decline limit pauses new assignments and forces you offline for the displayed cooldown. This does not remove government eligibility. Follow the app's Saba-local availability message before returning online.

An assigned delivery left untouched for **12 hours** is automatically returned to dispatch — a system cleanup, not a decline, so it does not count toward your limit. Deliveries already collected or part of a delivery run are never released this way.

## Collect and deliver all loads

Record **Water collected** for each physical load, choosing the actual fill station and checking the displayed assigned meter. Contact the office if the meter assignment is missing. One load is 1,000 gallons; a two-load request needs two collection records and 2,000 gallons delivered.

Use **Mark Delivered** only after the full requested quantity physically reaches the customer. Collection is not delivery, and there is no half-delivered request status. If you cannot finish, contact dispatch to coordinate the remaining work; do not mark the whole request delivered merely to clear your screen.

Delivered means your physical work on that request is complete. The resident's later confirmation or dispute is separate. You can receive another assignment when no claimed work remains and you meet the normal availability conditions; waiting for resident confirmation or an email does not hold you up.

## Delivery Runs

A dispatcher may deliberately assign several requests together as a Delivery Run. They appear as individual deliveries, possibly supported by a printed run sheet. Record each load's collection and each request's full delivery separately. There is no whole-run delivery button.

Normal assignments pause while claimed work remains in the run. Once physical work is finished, a run can still show **Awaiting Confirmation** without keeping you busy. If connectivity prevents app updates, coordinate with dispatch so staff can verify and record the work you actually completed. See [Dispatcher Guide](Dispatcher-Guide.md).
